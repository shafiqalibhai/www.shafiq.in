It’s the last day of 2013 and I’ve been sitting with the question of what I actually want to do differently in 2014. Write more, exercise more, read more, save more, learn a language, cut out social media, meditate every morning. By February I’d abandoned at least eight of them, and the ones I kept were half-hearted at best. The problem was never discipline. I made a WordPress theme that looks like iOS

I’ve been staring at my iPhone all day and I got tired of how every WordPress blog looks the same – flat headers, generic sidebars, the whole deal. So I spent the evening building a theme that actually looks like iOS, and I’m putting it up for anyone who wants it. The whole thing is built around that familiar iOS table view look. Striped background, rounded white cells with the little chevron arrows, a search bar at the top that matches the system aesthetic. It’s not trying to be a 1:1 copy of the iPhone – it’s just borrowing enough of the visual language that people who spend their days in iOS will feel at home. ...

Black & Blue — a free PSD template

This is a template I designed for Zap Web Online — a web solutions company that wanted a dark, techy look. The brief was fairly open: make it look fast, modern, and a bit futuristic. Hence the name. The layout is a fixed-width centre column on a textured metallic background. The header has the Zap Web logo on the left, a four-item navigation bar (Home, About Us, Portfolio, Contact) with blue highlight on the active tab, and a “Chat Live” button tucked into the top right of the banner.
