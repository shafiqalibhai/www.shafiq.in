I made a WordPress theme that looks like iOS

I’ve been staring at my iPhone all day and I got tired of how every WordPress blog looks the same – flat headers, generic sidebars, the whole deal. So I spent the evening building a theme that actually looks like iOS, and I’m putting it up for anyone who wants it. The whole thing is built around that familiar iOS table view look. Striped background, rounded white cells with the little chevron arrows, a search bar at the top that matches the system aesthetic. It’s not trying to be a 1:1 copy of the iPhone – it’s just borrowing enough of the visual language that people who spend their days in iOS will feel at home. ...
