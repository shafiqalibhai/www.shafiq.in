Notes on:

  • Communication: Effective communication strategies with partners, family, and friends.
  • Conflict resolution: Techniques for managing disagreements and strengthening relationships.
  • Support systems: Identifying and nurturing supportive relationships and social networks.

Quantified Self#

  • Time spent with family members
  • Quality time with spouse or partner
  • Dates or outings with friends
  • Social events attended
  • Phone calls made or received
  • Text messages sent or received
  • Video calls made or received
  • Gifts given or received
  • Acts of kindness performed
  • Social media interactions (likes, comments, shares)
  • New friendships formed
  • Relationship conflicts or disagreements
  • Resolved conflicts with loved ones
  • Family traditions upheld
  • Relationship goals progress
  • Love languages identified and practised
  • Personal boundaries maintained
  • Emotional support provided to others
  • Social skills practised or improved
  • Loneliness levels assessed

Family and Parenting#

Quality Time#

  • Quality time with children, spouse, parents, siblings
  • Family outings or activities planned
  • Family meetings held
  • Family traditions maintained
  • Parent-child bonding activities
  • Family meals enjoyed together
  • Household responsibilities shared
  • Family memories captured or recorded

Parenting#

  • Parenting challenges overcome
  • Parenting victories celebrated
  • Parenting goals progress
  • Family communication improved
  • Child development milestones tracked
  • Parenting resources consumed (books, podcasts, workshops)
  • Parenting philosophies explored
  • Parenting support networks engaged with

Small Talk Topics#
