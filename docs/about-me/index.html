About Me

17 June 2009 · Shafiq Alibhai

I’m Shafiq Alibhai. This site is where I keep my notes, rants, and the occasional useful thing I’ve figured out.

I’ve been running a homelab since before it was a thing to talk about, I bind my own books when I can’t find the edition I want, and I spend far too much time tweaking Linux distributions like they’re collectible cards.

Here are some things I enjoy:

Reading#

I read reference material, self-help books, and anything funny enough to make me snort in public. Digit and Chip magazines used to be my monthly ritual. Writing and Writers Digest taught me that most writing advice is just common sense wearing a tie.

Music#

I have no coherent taste. One minute I’m listening to Rahat Fateh Ali Khan, the next I’m in a Beyoncé deep cut, then suddenly it’s 50 Cent on repeat because why not. My actual favourites, the ones I keep coming back to:

Rahat Fateh Ali Khan, Barbara Pravi, Michael Jackson, Pharrell Williams, Shakira, Kylie Minogue, Gwen Stefani, Eric Clapton, Mariah Carey, Beyoncé, Camila Cabello, Amy Winehouse, Rihanna, Jennifer Lopez, Adele, Usher, Avril Lavigne, John Mayer, Dua Lipa, 50 Cent, Eminem, Shania Twain, Bryan Adams, Nelly Furtado, Ricky Martin, Lalisa, Lily Allen, Robbie Williams, Elton John.

Screens#

I play Quake 3, Unreal Tournament, Road Rash, and Need for Speed. I watch Formula 1. That’s it. That’s the list.

Software#

I use the tools that get out of my way:

  • Zed for editing
  • Git for version control
  • Homebrew, Snapcraft, Flatpak for getting things installed
  • Waterfox for browsing
  • Hugo for this site
  • Pandoc and LaTeX when I need to produce something that looks proper
  • Lima / Docker / Podman for containers
  • Alacritty as my terminal
  • VLC and Kodi for media
  • The Unarchiver because macOS still can’t open a RAR
  • Lulu for firewall rules
  • LibreOffice because proprietary office suites are a tax on productivity
  • Macs Fan Control because stock thermal management is a suggestion, not a guarantee

I’ve tried every Ubuntu flavour, plus PostmarketOS, Mobian, Armbian, Droidian, and openFyde. I collect operating systems the way other people collect shoes.

People#

Lewis Hamilton, Karun Chandok, Barack Obama, Jay Shetty, Romesh Ranganathan, Jimmy Carr, Robert Kiyosaki, Steven Bartlett, Dr Rangan Chatterjee, Naomi Schiff, Michael Jackson, Remo Fernandes, Sadiq Khan, Jack Kelly.

No particular order. Some I admire, some I find interesting, some I just enjoy listening to.

Doing Good#

I donate to Cancer Research UK, Barnardo’s, the British Heart Foundation, Midlands Air Ambulance Charity Shop, and The Salvation Army in West Bromwich and Oldbury. Buying second-hand from the Salvation Army isn’t just charity for me – it’s also a decent way to furnish a flat.

Politics#

I follow what’s happening in the UK and the European Union. Not passionately, but I pay attention.

Writing#

I’ve written about a lot of things: fitness, spirituality, personal development, social issues, self-care, self-promotion at work, and even an Ansible for Windows guide and a Terraform write-up. I also wrote Your Playbook for an Amazing Life. Some of it is useful. Some of it is just me thinking out loud.
