Resources for International Trade in the UK

Table of Contents
  1. Training and courses
  2. Customs and compliance
  3. Government resources
  4. Video content

I’ve been looking into international trade for the UK and collected a few useful resources along the way. Nothing original, just links that might help someone starting out.

Training and courses#

  • Think Sandwell – International Trade – A Typeform questionnaire from the Think Sandwell programme. Seems aimed at businesses in the West Midlands looking to get into export.

  • Black Country Chamber – International Trade Training – Structured courses on the mechanics of trading internationally. Covers things like documentation, shipping, and compliance.

  • Black Country Chamber – International Trade – General guidance and resources from the same chamber. Good starting point if you’re based in the Black Country area.

Customs and compliance#

  • Chamber Customs – A consultancy that seems to specialise in customs procedures. Useful if you’re stuck on post-Brexit import/export paperwork.

Government resources#

  • HMRC – Importing and Exporting Guidance – Official HMRC page with alerts, videos, and webinars. Not the most exciting read but it’s the primary source for anything regulatory.

Video content#

  • YouTube playlist on international trade – A playlist covering various trade topics. Worth a watch if you learn better from video than from government guidance.
