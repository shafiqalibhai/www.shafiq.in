One resolution for 2014

Table of Contents
  1. Blog more often

It’s the last day of 2013 and I’ve been sitting with the question of what I actually want to do differently in 2014. Not what I should do, not what sounds good on a vision board, but what I genuinely want to change about how I spend my time.

I’ve made lists before. Five resolutions, ten resolutions, the whole lot. Write more, exercise more, read more, save more, learn a language, cut out social media, meditate every morning. By February I’d abandoned at least eight of them, and the ones I kept were half-hearted at best. The problem was never discipline. It was that I was trying to change everything at once, which is just a fancy way of saying I wasn’t going to change anything at all.

So this year I’m doing one thing.

Blog more often#

That’s it. One resolution. Write here more often.

I’ve had this blog for years and it’s become a graveyard of half-finished tutorials and one-off problem solutions. There’s nothing wrong with that, honestly, but I’ve been thinking about what I actually want this space to be. Not a portfolio, not a documentation dump, but somewhere I can work through ideas properly. The kind of place where I can take a thought that’s been bouncing around my head for weeks and finally give it structure, even if nobody reads it.

I think the barrier has always been the blank page. Not a lack of things to say, but the pressure of saying something worth saying. Every post feels like it needs to be useful, or clever, or at least coherent. So I don’t write. And then another month passes and the gap feels too big to close.

What if I just wrote? Not for an audience, not for clout, not even for myself in some enlightened sense. Just because the act of putting words on a page tends to sort the thinking out whether I feel ready or not. Some of the best ideas I’ve had came after I’d already started writing and hadn’t planned where they were going.

I don’t have a target. No “three posts a week” or “daily writing”. I’m just going to write when I have something to say and stop making excuses when I don’t. If that means a post every two weeks, fine. If it means three in a month and then silence for a quarter, also fine. The resolution isn’t about output. It’s about removing the friction that’s kept me from writing for too long.

That’s all. One thing. Let’s see what happens.
