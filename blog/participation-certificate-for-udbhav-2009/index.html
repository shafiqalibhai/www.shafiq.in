Participation Certificate for Udbhav 2009

22 March 2009 · Shafiq Alibhai

A participation certificate for Udbhav 2009. Same design problem as the Respograph one — make it feel official without looking like a template. This one had to sit within the wider Udbhav visual identity, so the palette and typography carried through from the posters and banners.

This was part of a larger design effort for Udbhav 2009 that also included posters, flyers, stickers, mini banners, the souvenir book cover, and the stage backdrop.
