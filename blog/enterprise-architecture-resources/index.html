I’ve been collecting these over the years. Some are worth more than others, and a few have gone stale, but they’re the ones I keep coming back to.

Agile Architect#

Andrew Johnston’s blog is one of the few places that actually talks about agile in enterprise architecture without treating the two as separate worlds. agilearchitect.org

Application Development Trends#

ADT Magazine has an enterprise section that covers the news and trends. It’s not EA-specific but it’s useful for keeping an eye on what’s moving. adtmag.com

Architecture & Governance Magazine#

Governance is the part of EA that most people get wrong, and this magazine at least takes it seriously. architectureandgovernance.com

Business Rules Group#

If you’re interested in the business side rather than the IT side, this is where to look. businessrulesgroup.org

Cutter Consortium#

Research organisation with some useful EA insights, though most of it is behind a paywall. cutter.com

EWITA#

Enterprise-Wide IT Architecture. A bit older but still has some solid content on the practical side of things. ewita.com

Forrester Research#

Heavy on the EA and CIO content, but again, most of it requires a subscription. Worth browsing if you have access. forrester.com

IFEAD#

The Institute for Enterprise Architecture Developments was founded by Jaap Schekkerman. More academic than most of the rest on this list. nascio.org

Bredemeyer Consulting#

Bob Bredemeyer’s resources for software architects. Practical, not theoretical. bredemeyer.com

Software Engineering Institute#

CMU’s SEI has guidelines, best practices, and training material. The formal side of things, but useful when you need it. sei.cmu.edu

The Open Group Architecture Forum#

This is where TOGAF lives. If you’re working with or against that framework, this is the place to be. thegroup.org

Zachman Institute#

The Zachman Framework isn’t going anywhere. ZIFA keeps it alive. zifa.com
